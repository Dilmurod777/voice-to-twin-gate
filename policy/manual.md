# Fuel filter replacement

Written for this project after the fuel-system chapter of *FAA-H-8083-31B, Aviation
Maintenance Technician Handbook — Airframe* (US Government work, public domain). The
sequence is standard practice; the wording is ours, so no maintenance manual is reproduced.

Prerequisites: parking brake set, aircraft on jacks.

1. Open access panel 12A on the left wing root.
2. Depressurise the fuel system at the drain valve.
3. Open the fuel shut-off valve.
4. Remove the fuel filter element.
5. Fit a new element and leak-check the housing.
6. Remove the hydraulic pump for the return-to-service check.
